PRODUCT DOCUMENTATION & INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

Project Preview

This portfolio entry summarizes my work on Dell's knowledge base article How to identify AI-enabled Windows computers, aimed at helping customers and support teams quickly determine whether a device meets AI PC criteria and where to find the relevant specs and signals within Dell's ecosystem.

View Official KB

Context

As part of Dell Technologies’ product content initiatives, I focused on clarifying and structuring documentation around emerging AI PC terminology and capabilities. The objective was to provide clear guidance for enterprise IT teams and end users on understanding what qualifies as an AI-enabled Windows computer and how to locate those details across Dell product pages and support tools.

Problem

With rapid changes in hardware (CPU, GPU, NPU) and OS features, customers needed a single, authoritative resource that explains the indicators of AI capability and where to verify them. Prior content was fragmented or overly technical, creating confusion and additional load on support channels.

Contribution

  • Conducted a terminology and content audit across related Dell KBs and product specs.
  • Defined an IA that maps hardware capabilities (CPU/GPU/NPU) to Windows AI features.
  • Wrote scannable guidance with headings, steps, and definitions for quick decisions.
  • Aligned UI and wording with product and support teams for consistency and accuracy.
  • Prepared the content for localization and iterative updates as AI PC criteria evolve.

Impact

The article improves customer self-service, searchability, and support accuracy by centralizing AI PC identification guidance. It provides a reusable structure for future AI-related product content and reduces ambiguity across documentation surfaces.
